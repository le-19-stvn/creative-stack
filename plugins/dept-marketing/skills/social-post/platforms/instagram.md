# Instagram — channel notes

Read under the `social-post` law: this file governs **form only**. The claim, audience, enemy, proof and voice come from `POSITIONING.md` unchanged.

Here the visual carries the argument and the caption supports it — the inverse of every other channel in this skill.

## Rules first

- **Disclose partnerships and paid content.** Plainly, in the post itself.
- **No stock imagery posing as the real thing.** Not as the product, not as customers, not as the team.
- **Write alt text.** Every image, every carousel slide. Accessibility isn't optional and isn't a growth tactic.
- **No hashtag walls.** A short set that genuinely describes the post, or none.

## What the form rewards (durable mechanics)

- **The visual states the idea; it doesn't decorate it.** If the image would work under any other caption, the post has no argument.
- **Carousel: one idea per slide, and slide 1 states the idea** — it doesn't withhold it. A first slide that teases is a first slide that gets scrolled past.
- **The last slide is the actual next step**, named concretely.
- **The caption's first line is the only guaranteed read.** Put the specific claim there, not context.
- **Visual consistency is the brand's memory here.** Same system across posts.

## Anti-patterns — reject on sight

- The motivational quote card.
- "Link in bio" as the only CTA, with no reason to follow it.
- A carousel whose slides restate one thought five ways to hit a slide count.
- Aesthetic that belongs to the template, not the brand.

## Hand the visual to design

If the design department is mounted, visual direction belongs to it and to `BRAND.md` — hand it over rather than inventing a look here. If it isn't mounted, describe the visual precisely enough to be produced, and stay inside whatever `BRAND.md` or `TASTE.md` already declares. Never invent a visual identity from this file.
