I need the hero visual for our landing page made this week, and I'm handing the execution to someone else. Write the brief they'll work from.

Our `TASTE.md`:

```
# Taste — Ledgerline

## References
- Stripe docs — dense but never cramped; the type does the hierarchy, not boxes.
- My accountant's paper worksheets — numbers aligned so the eye checks them fast.

## Anti-references
- Every French fintech landing page: illustration of a smiling person holding a card.

## Feeling
Should evoke: precise, quiet, trustworthy.
Must NOT evoke: playful, disruptive, friendly-startup.

## What We Explicitly Reject
- No centered hero with the headline in the middle and the CTA underneath.
- No illustrated characters, ever.
- No rounded corners above 4px.

## Non-negotiables
- The accent is the ink blue from my letterhead: #1B2A4A. One accent, no second.
- Body type is a real text face, never a UI sans.

## What I bring
Nine years doing this job. I know the three numbers a bookkeeper checks first.
```

Our `BRAND.md`:

```
# Brand — Ledgerline

## Palette
Ink #1B2A4A (accent, one only) · Paper #FBFAF7 · Graphite #2E2E2E (text) · Rule #DCD7CC (hairlines)

## Typography
Display: Söhne Halbfett, 48-72px. Body: Source Serif 4, 17px/1.6. Numerals: tabular, always.

## Logo
Wordmark only, flat, no gradient ever. Never below 24px.

## Tone of voice
Flat, technical, no exclamation marks.

## What We Explicitly Reject
- No drop shadows; depth comes from the paper and rule layers.
- Never set a number in a lighter weight than its label.
```
